# ADR-0012: Tune story difficulty: roaming pool pacing and aspect duel budgets

## Status

accepted (auto-accepted, unreviewed)

**Domains**:

## Context

Difficulty telemetry showed solo roaming duels ending in 1-2 rounds (the mow-down) while the Bargaining and Depression aspects beat full-pools protagonists 9/10 and 8/10 via condition tracks; both extremes came from pool budgets, not potency

## Decision

Tune story difficulty: roaming pool pacing and aspect duel budgets

## Consequences

Story roaming mobs carry 1.5x pools (1.25x on the tutorial floor) so duels take 2-4 contested rounds; Bargaining 3.0->2.5 and Depression 2.5->2.0 pool factors make their outlast/grind winnable; tower curve unchanged at 1.0; pack-of-3 fights remain a deliberate worst case a Fighter should not face at parity
