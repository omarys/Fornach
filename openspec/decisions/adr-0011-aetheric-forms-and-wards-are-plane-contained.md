# ADR-0011: Aetheric forms and wards are plane-contained

## Status

accepted (auto-accepted, unreviewed)

**Domains**:

## Context

A Fighter answering a mental foe was handed AegisLattice/PhantasmalDiffusion by resolveActiveDefense and Acumen-scaled ward by CenterMind; the form is invisible on a physical sheet, so its +15/turn upkeep fed a ward the player could not see or spend (reported as ward climbing on every action). resolveThreadComplexForm already refused non-Mental actors; the remaining writers now follow the same rule.

## Decision

Aetheric forms and wards are plane-contained

## Consequences

Physical actors vent the same mental meters but raise no form, clones or ward; applyTurnUpkeep self-heals stale forms off a physical sheet; chooseActiveDefense keys on the actor's plane, not the opponent's
