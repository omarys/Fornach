# ADR-0002: Prowess Poise and Asymmetric Tempo Redesign

## Status

accepted

**Domains**: unknown

## Context

Prowess attacks decluttered into passive intent-reading and attack bonuses; Poise converted to defense bonus and pain/strain resistance. Active choices streamlined to Power and Agility vectors dynamically bound to Vor/Nach tempo roles, with bestiary-driven discipline progression and status meter primacy.

## Decision

The system SHALL treat Prowess and Poise as passive attributes and restrict active combat choices to Power and Agility vectors dynamically bound to Vor and Nach tempo roles.

## Consequences

Prowess and Poise removed from active combat menus and manual level-up stat allocations. Focus resource retired. Beast combat identity refactored with Fortitude fallback and limp-away mechanics. Damage reduced by 30% to prioritize tactical meter collapses.
