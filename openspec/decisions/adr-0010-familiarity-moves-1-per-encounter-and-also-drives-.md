# ADR-0010: Familiarity moves 1% per encounter and also drives riposte/disarm

## Status

accepted (auto-accepted, unreviewed)

**Domains**:

## Context

A five-encounter climb to mastery made the +/-25% species curve too shallow to matter and left the reactive counter unscaled; one curve should govern knowledge everywhere.

## Decision

Familiarity moves 1% per encounter and also drives riposte/disarm

## Consequences

Fifty encounters now separate a first meeting from mastery, so a species met once stays at -25% for the whole run; scrutiny bonuses in TacticalAssessment remain additive and uncapped.
