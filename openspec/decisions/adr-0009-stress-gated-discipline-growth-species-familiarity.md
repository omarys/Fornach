# ADR-0009: Stress-gated discipline growth, species familiarity damage curve, physical-only combat ramps

## Status

accepted

**Domains**: combat, progression

## Context

Discipline was growing free (+1 per encounter) and compounding off every meter, so nothing marked mastery. Growth now demands pressure: +5 for a victory paid in blood/morale/status, +1 above half Recklessness, +10 on defeat; bestiary familiarity replaces the flat grant as the repeatable axis, scaling damage 0.75->1.25 dealt (mirror on taken) over the first five encounters per species. Meters ramp only Power/Agility stats - movement - while Prowess/Poise/Acumen/Composure stay flat in combat.

## Decision

The system SHALL gate discipline growth behind encounter stress and defeat, scale combat damage with species familiarity across the first five encounters, and restrict combat stat ramps to physical movement attributes.

## Consequences

Early floors are measurably harder for unfamiliar species (OptimalPlay baselines re-pinned: Fighter 1.67, Rogue 0.83, warden prologue 7/10; Depression aspect 3/8 seeds). One-off story bosses stay at the full penalty for the whole duel - defeat's +10 growth is the intended ladder rung. Mental defences (Resolve/Intuition) keep their per-encounter +1 tick.
