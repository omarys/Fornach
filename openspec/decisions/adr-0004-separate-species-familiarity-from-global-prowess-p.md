# ADR-0004: Separate Species Familiarity from Global Prowess Progression

## Status

accepted

**Domains**: unknown

## Context

Global Prowess inflating by 25+ points per encounter trivialized subsequent fights across Floor 1. Tracking global Prowess at +1 and per-species familiarity separately at 5-3-1 preserves progression balance.

## Decision

The system SHALL award +1 global Prowess per encounter while tracking per-species familiarity separately via BestiaryKnowledge using a 5-3-1 progression.

## Consequences

Baseline Prowess remains modest (+1/fight) while tactical advantages against known enemy types scale smoothly via BestiaryKnowledge.
