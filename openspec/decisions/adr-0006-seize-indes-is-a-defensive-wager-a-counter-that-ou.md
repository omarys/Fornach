# ADR-0006: Seize Indes is a defensive wager: a counter that out-rolls the strike stops it and takes the Vor, one that loses lands 1.5x and spends every banked stack

## Status

accepted

**Domains**: unknown

## Context

No defensive option let a player take the initiative from the Nach on their own terms, and mitigation cannot save you from a strike that outclasses you outright. A counter contests a different stat pair, so it stays meaningful at any tier disparity.

## Decision

The system SHALL resolve a Seize Indes counter by negating the incoming strike and granting the Vor on a winning contest, while inflicting 1.5x damage and expending all banked ramp stacks on a loss.

## Consequences

Losing costs more than bracing: 1.5x damage plus the whole ramp. Enemy AI does not use it, so simulated balance is unchanged; it adds a player option, not a difficulty term. Documented in docs/adr/0010-seize-indes-as-a-defensive-wager.md.
