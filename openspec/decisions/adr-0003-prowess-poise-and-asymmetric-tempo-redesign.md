# ADR-0003: Prowess Poise and Asymmetric Tempo Redesign

## Status

accepted

**Domains**: Combat, Bestiary

## Context

Streamline combat options to Power and Agility vectors bound dynamically to Vor and Nach tempo roles, convert Prowess and Poise to passive reading and contest determining factors, replace legacy mastery multipliers with bestiary knowledge progression, add pain shock checks, and establish status meter primacy.

## Decision

The system SHALL resolve combat actions via dynamic Vor and Nach tempo roles using Power and Agility vectors, treating Prowess and Poise as passive contest factors influenced by bestiary knowledge progression.

## Consequences

Active Prowess and Focus retired; humanoid-only Prowess/Poise; active defenses FortitudeGuard and ReflexSlip counter offensive strikes in Nach; beasts limp away when wounded.
