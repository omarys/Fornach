# Fornach-domain Specification

> Generated by openlore v1.2.0 on 2026-10-07 14:42

## Purpose

Manages combat gauges and operational stress meters, enforcing boundaries and state band
categorizations.

## Requirements

> `src/Fornach.Domain/Identifiers.fs`

### Requirement: CreateMeter

The system SHALL initializes a meter bounded strictly between 0 and 100 percent.

#### Scenario: ClampingOutOfBoundsMeterValue
- **GIVEN** A raw integer value exceeding 100 or below 0
- **WHEN** Create is invoked
- **THEN** The meter value MUST be clamped between 0 and 100

### Requirement: CategorizeMeterBand

The system SHALL categorizes a meter's current strain value into operational bands (normal, strained, critical, collapsed).

#### Scenario: MeterReachedMaximumStrain
- **GIVEN** A meter with value 100
- **WHEN** Evaluated against meter patterns
- **THEN** It MUST return the Collapsed active pattern band

#### Scenario: MeterInCriticalStrain
- **GIVEN** A meter with value between 75 and 99
- **WHEN** Evaluated against meter patterns
- **THEN** It MUST return the Critical active pattern band

### Requirement: CreatePool

The system SHALL creates a new resource pool initialized at maximum capacity, with a minimum maximum of 1.

#### Scenario: InitializePoolWithPositiveMax
<!-- openlore-test: tags=smoke (auto) -->
- **GIVEN** A maximum capacity value greater than zero
- **WHEN** Create is called
- **THEN** A Pool MUST be created where Current equals Maximum

#### Scenario: InitializePoolWithInvalidMax
<!-- openlore-test: tags=regression (auto) -->
- **GIVEN** A maximum capacity value less than 1
- **WHEN** Create is called
- **THEN** The pool Maximum MUST default to 1

### Requirement: ApplyPoolDelta

The system SHALL applies an increment or decrement to current resources, clamping between 0 and maximum.

#### Scenario: DepletingPoolResources
- **GIVEN** A pool with current resources
- **WHEN** A negative delta exceeding current capacity is applied
- **THEN** Current MUST be clamped to 0 and IsDepleted MUST be true

#### Scenario: OverhealingPoolResources
- **GIVEN** A pool with current resources below maximum
- **WHEN** A positive delta exceeding maximum is applied
- **THEN** Current MUST be clamped to Maximum

### Requirement: AssessCombatant

The system SHALL compares observer scrutiny against target masking composure to derive tactical insight level, primary stats, openings, and strategic advice.

#### Scenario: PenetratingInsightAchieved
- **GIVEN** An observer whose effective scrutiny ratio against the target's composure is 1.15 or higher
- **WHEN** assess is called with the observer and target
- **THEN** The assessment MUST have Penetrating insight level, revealing exact primary stat, apex strength,
critical flaw defense, and specific counter advice

#### Scenario: KeenInsightAchieved
- **GIVEN** An observer whose effective scrutiny ratio against the target's composure is between 0.75 and 1.15
- **WHEN** assess is called with the observer and target
- **THEN** The assessment MUST have Keen insight level, detailing exact stat values and identified defensive
openings

#### Scenario: DiscerningInsightAchieved
- **GIVEN** An observer whose effective scrutiny ratio against the target's composure is between 0.40 and 0.75
- **WHEN** assess is called with the observer and target
- **THEN** The assessment MUST have Discerning insight level, showing estimated stat ranges and general
tactical tendencies

#### Scenario: ObscuredInsightDueToHighComposure
- **GIVEN** An observer whose effective scrutiny ratio against the target's composure is below 0.40
- **WHEN** assess is called with the observer and target
- **THEN** The assessment MUST have Obscured insight level with masked exact stats and advice to build Study
Stacks

### Requirement: CalculateEffectiveScrutiny

The system SHALL calculates total scrutiny rating combining acumen, intuition perceptual bonus, and accumulated study stacks.

#### Scenario: ComputeScrutinyWithStudyStacks
- **GIVEN** A combatant with base Acumen, Intuition, and active Study Stacks
- **WHEN** calculateEffectiveScrutiny is called
- **THEN** It SHALL return at least 1, summing Acumen, a quarter of Intuition, and 15 points per Study Stack

### Requirement: CalculateEffectiveMasking

The system SHALL calculates concealing composure stat used by a target combatant to hide attributes.

#### Scenario: ComputeComposureMasking
- **GIVEN** A combatant with a Composure stat
- **WHEN** calculateEffectiveMasking is called
- **THEN** It SHALL return the Composure value or at minimum 1

### Requirement: AnalyzeTargetStats

The system SHALL identifies the highest stat, best offense, best defense, and weakest defense corresponding to the combatant's plane.

#### Scenario: AnalyzePhysicalPlaneStats
- **GIVEN** A target combatant residing on the Physical plane
- **WHEN** analyzeTargetStats is called
- **THEN** It SHALL evaluate Force, Fortitude, Finesse, Reflex, Prowess, and Poise to determine peak and
weakest attributes

#### Scenario: AnalyzeMentalPlaneStats
- **GIVEN** A target combatant residing on the Mental plane
- **WHEN** analyzeTargetStats is called
- **THEN** It SHALL evaluate Intellect, Resolve, Acuity, Intuition, Acumen, and Composure to determine peak and
weakest attributes

### Requirement: GenerateAdvice

The system SHALL generates strategic counter-tactics based on target primary threat, defensive openings, and achieved insight tier.

#### Scenario: GenerateActionableAdviceOnVulnerableDefense
- **GIVEN** Keen or Penetrating insight with an identified weakest defense stat
- **WHEN** generateAdvice is called
- **THEN** It SHALL format tailored advice advising strikes targeting that specific defense type

### Requirement: Resolve

The system SHALL calculates the active visioncone for a combatant by evaluating locomotion arc base, discipline bonuses, entropy meter penalties, and environmental sight radius attenuation.

#### Scenario: WalkingInClearEnvironment
- **GIVEN** A combatant walking with baseline meters in clear lighting and weather
- **WHEN** resolve is called
- **THEN** A VisionCone is returned with a 120-degree base arc adjusted by discipline bonus and meter
penalties, and calculated sight radius

#### Scenario: SprintingArcConstriction
- **GIVEN** A combatant sprinting with high agility (Reflex and Finesse)
- **WHEN** resolve is called
- **THEN** The vision arc constricts to a forward cone between 60 and 110 degrees based on agility bonus

#### Scenario: StationaryAwarenessDilation
- **GIVEN** A combatant stationary for multiple resting turns with high Poise
- **WHEN** resolve is called
- **THEN** The vision arc dilates outward up to 360 degrees and omnidirectional facing (None) is applied if
reaching 360 degrees

#### Scenario: EnvironmentalAndMeterDegradation
- **GIVEN** A combatant with elevated Overwhelm, Recklessness, and Exhaustion in low light or dense weather
- **WHEN** resolve is called
- **THEN** The visual arc is clamped to a minimum of 30 degrees and the sight radius is attenuated by
exhaustion and environmental factors to at least 1
