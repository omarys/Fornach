# Fornach-domain Specification

> Generated by openlore v1.2.0 on 2026-11-13 21:11

## Purpose

Calculates proportional attribute bonuses as a percentage of current values, guaranteeing minimum
scaling thresholds.

## Requirements

### Requirement: PercentOf

The system SHALL the system shall calculate a bonus as a percentage of the current stat value, rounding half away from zero and guaranteeing a minimum value of 1.

#### Scenario: StandardPercentageCalculation
- **GIVEN** A current value of 100 and a percentage bonus of 10
- **WHEN** percentOf is evaluated
- **THEN** The returned bonus SHALL be 10

#### Scenario: RoundingHalfAwayFromZero
- **GIVEN** A current value of 45 and a percentage bonus of 33
- **WHEN** percentOf is evaluated
- **THEN** The returned bonus SHALL be rounded half away from zero to 15

#### Scenario: EnforceMinimumBonusFloor
- **GIVEN** A current value of 2 and a percentage bonus of 1
- **WHEN** percentOf is evaluated
- **THEN** The returned bonus SHALL be clamped to a minimum of 1
