# Fornach-cli Specification

> Generated by openlore v1.2.0 on 2026-10-07 14:42

## Purpose

Renders terminal visual effects and replay animations for player death sequences.

## Requirements

> `src/Fornach.Cli/DeathAnimation.fs`

### Requirement: PlayTruckReplay

The system SHALL render a multi-phase animated truck-kun replay sequence across the terminal width, including truck approach, impact collision, screen flash, and atmospheric fade.

#### Scenario: RenderDeathReplayAnimationSequence
- **GIVEN** The terminal console is active and accessible via Spectre.Console
- **WHEN** playTruckReplay is called upon player death
- **THEN** The console SHALL clear, animate the vehicle approach and collision frames across the terminal
width, flash a white screen, and display concluding atmospheric text before restoring the cursor

## Technical Notes

- **Dependencies**: Spectre.Console
