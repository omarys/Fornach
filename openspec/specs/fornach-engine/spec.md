# Fornach-engine Specification

> Generated by openlore v1.2.0 on 2026-10-19 15:43

## Purpose

Encapsulates combat resolution logic for the Fornach engine, including action execution, contest
dice rolling, damage and mitigation calculations (armor, arcane ward, stances, tinctures, monster
traits), status effect management, turn upkeep, and multi-target group mechanics (cleave, chained
strikes, and area-of-effect psychic splash).

### Requirement: ComputeTierMultiplier

The system SHALL the service shall calculate a lethality multiplier based on net hits from a combat contest, mapping net hits from 0 or below to 0.0 up to a tiered cap of 5.0.

#### Scenario: ZeroOrNegativeNetHits
- **GIVEN** A contest outcome with netHits less than or equal to 0
- **WHEN** computeTierMultiplier is called
- **THEN** The returned multiplier MUST be 0.0

#### Scenario: StandardTierScaling
- **GIVEN** A contest outcome with 5 net hits
- **WHEN** computeTierMultiplier is called
- **THEN** The returned multiplier MUST be 2.20

#### Scenario: HighNetHitsCap
- **GIVEN** A contest outcome with 15 net hits
- **WHEN** computeTierMultiplier is called
- **THEN** The returned multiplier MUST be capped at 5.00

### Requirement: Resolve

The system SHALL the service shall resolve a combat action intent between an actor and target with zero prior defenses, applying turn upkeep, verifying discipline permissions, executing the action (attack, recovery, stance shift, form thread, or preparation deployment), and recording all combat events.

#### Scenario: UpkeepDepletionHaltsAction
- **GIVEN** An actor whose Health is depleted by start-of-turn upkeep
- **WHEN** resolve is called
- **THEN** The action terminates immediately and returns only the upkeep events without resolving the intended
action

#### Scenario: StandardAttackResolution
- **GIVEN** A healthy actor and target with an allowed standard attack intent
- **WHEN** resolve is called
- **THEN** Contest dice are rolled, net hits and damage mitigation are evaluated, status meters are updated,
and an ActionResult with updated combatants and events is returned

#### Scenario: DisallowedAttackPlaneRestriction
- **GIVEN** A physical-discipline combatant attempting to execute a magic/mental attack
- **WHEN** resolve is called
- **THEN** The attack is disallowed, emitting a ComboReset event with no contest executed

The system SHALL the system shall calculate a bounded dice pool size between 4 and 20 dice based on the ratio of the participant's stat to the opposing stat.

#### Scenario: ScaledPoolCalculation
- **GIVEN** A participant's stat value and an opponent's stat value

#### Scenario: MinimumPoolBoundaryClamping
- **GIVEN** A stat value substantially lower than the opposing stat
- **WHEN** computePoolSize is invoked
- **THEN** The returned pool size is clamped to the minimum of 4

#### Scenario: MaximumPoolBoundaryClamping
- **GIVEN** A stat value substantially higher than the opposing stat
- **WHEN** computePoolSize is invoked
- **THEN** The returned pool size is clamped to the maximum of 20

### Requirement: ComputeFloorHits

The system SHALL the system shall calculate deterministic floor hits, guaranteeing one automatic hit for every 15 points in the evaluated stat.

#### Scenario: StatMeetsOrExceedsFloorHitThreshold
- **GIVEN** A stat value of at least 15
- **WHEN** computeFloorHits is invoked
- **THEN** One hit is granted per full 15 stat points

#### Scenario: StatBelowFloorHitThreshold
- **GIVEN** A stat value less than 15
- **WHEN** computeFloorHits is invoked
- **THEN** Zero floor hits are granted

### Requirement: EvaluatePool

The system SHALL the system shall evaluate a combatant's dice pool for a specified vector (power, agility, or discipline), applying vector-specific target numbers, rerolls from study stacks, deterministic floor hits, and detecting glitch conditions.

#### Scenario: PowerVectorEvaluation
- **GIVEN** A participant rolling using the Power vector
- **WHEN** evaluatePool is invoked
- **THEN** Dice rolling 5 count as 1 hit and dice rolling 6 count as 2 hits, added to deterministic floor hits

#### Scenario: AgilityVectorEvaluation
- **GIVEN** A participant rolling using the Agility vector
- **WHEN** evaluatePool is invoked
- **THEN** Dice rolling 4 or higher count as 1 hit each, added to deterministic floor hits

#### Scenario: DisciplineVectorEvaluationWithStudyRerolls
- **GIVEN** A participant rolling using the Discipline vector with available study stacks
- **WHEN** evaluatePool is invoked
- **THEN** Up to studyStacks dice showing 1 are rerolled before counting hits with target number 4 or higher

#### Scenario: GlitchDetection
- **GIVEN** A roll outcome where more than half of the final dice show 1
- **WHEN** evaluatePool is invoked
- **THEN** The result records IsGlitch as true
