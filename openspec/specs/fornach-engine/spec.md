hook triggers during combat action resolution.

### Requirement: FlankDamageMultiplier

The system SHALL the service shall calculate the incoming damage multiplier based on the attacker's relative flank orientation (1.0x for front, 1.15x for flank, and 1.30x for rear).

#### Scenario: FrontalAttackMultiplier
- **GIVEN** An incoming attack arriving head-on
- **WHEN** flankDamageMultiplier is called with Flank.Front
- **THEN** The returned damage multiplier MUST be 1.0

#### Scenario: FlankAttackMultiplier
- **GIVEN** An incoming attack arriving from the side
- **WHEN** flankDamageMultiplier is called with Flank.Flank
- **THEN** The returned damage multiplier MUST be 1.15

#### Scenario: RearAttackMultiplier
- **GIVEN** An incoming attack arriving from behind
- **WHEN** flankDamageMultiplier is called with Flank.Rear
- **THEN** The returned damage multiplier MUST be 1.30

### Requirement: FlankOf

The system SHALL the service shall determine the attacking combatant's flank position relative to the player's current facing direction and grid position, defaulting to front if facing or position is unknown.

#### Scenario: AttackerPositionedBehindFacingPlayer
- **GIVEN** A player facing North at (0, 0) and an enemy at (0, -1)
- **WHEN** flankOf is evaluated for the enemy's CombatantId
- **THEN** The returned flank classification MUST be Flank.Rear

#### Scenario: PlayerHasNoFacingDirection
- **GIVEN** A player state where Facing is None
- **WHEN** flankOf is evaluated for an enemy
- **THEN** The returned flank classification MUST default to Flank.Front

### Requirement: UpdateFov

The system SHALL the service shall recalculate the player's field of view cone, mark newly visible floor tiles, update the explored tile set, and register newly spotted enemies.

#### Scenario: RecalculateFOVAndRevealTiles
- **GIVEN** A TowerRunState with unexplored tiles and unseen enemies within the player's line of sight
- **WHEN** updateFov is called
- **THEN** The CurrentFloor's Visible tiles MUST reflect the computed FOV cone
- **AND** Explored tiles MUST contain the union of previous explored tiles and visible tiles
- **AND** SeenEnemies MUST include all living enemy IDs present in the visible tiles

### Requirement: FocusOnWait

The system SHALL the service shall increment the player's stationary focus depth by one turn when resting or waiting, widening the vision cone.

#### Scenario: IncrementFocusDepthFromWalking
- **GIVEN** A locomotion state of Walking (0 focus turns)
- **WHEN** focusOnWait is called
- **THEN** The returned LocomotionState MUST be Stationary with 1 focus turn

#### Scenario: IncrementExistingStationaryFocus
- **GIVEN** A locomotion state of Stationary with 2 focus turns
- **WHEN** focusOnWait is called
- **THEN** The returned LocomotionState MUST be Stationary with 3 focus turns

### Requirement: FocusOnMove

The system SHALL the service shall decrement the player's stationary focus depth when moving, or transition locomotion back to walking once focus depth reaches baseline.

#### Scenario: ReduceHighFocusDepthOnMovement
- **GIVEN** A locomotion state of Stationary with 3 focus turns
- **WHEN** focusOnMove is called
- **THEN** The returned LocomotionState MUST be Stationary with 2 focus turns

#### Scenario: TransitionToWalkingWhenFocusReachesBaseline
- **GIVEN** A locomotion state of Stationary with 1 focus turn
- **WHEN** focusOnMove is called
- **THEN** The returned LocomotionState MUST be LocomotionState.Walking

### Requirement: RestRecoverHealth

The system SHALL the service shall recover health towards the rest cap (60% of maximum health) without exceeding it or reducing current health.

#### Scenario: RecoverHealthBelowRestCap
- **GIVEN** A health pool with Maximum 100 and Current 40 (rest cap is 60)
- **WHEN** restRecoverHealth is called with an amount of 15
- **THEN** The updated pool Current health MUST be 55

#### Scenario: CapHealthRecoveryAt60Percent
- **GIVEN** A health pool with Maximum 100 and Current 55 (rest cap is 60)
- **WHEN** restRecoverHealth is called with an amount of 20
- **THEN** The updated pool Current health MUST be capped at 60

#### Scenario: HealthAlreadyAtOrAboveRestCap
- **GIVEN** A health pool with Maximum 100 and Current 75
- **WHEN** restRecoverHealth is called with an amount of 10
- **THEN** The updated pool Current health MUST remain 75

### Requirement: CloseBleed

The system SHALL the service shall decrement active bleed stacks by the closed amount, flooring the result at zero.

#### Scenario: ReduceBleedStacks
- **GIVEN** A combatant with 3 bleed stacks
- **WHEN** closeBleed is called with closed amount 1
- **THEN** The remaining bleed stacks MUST be 2

#### Scenario: CloseBleedFlooredAtZero
- **GIVEN** A combatant with 2 bleed stacks
- **WHEN** closeBleed is called with closed amount 5
- **THEN** The remaining bleed stacks MUST be 0

### Requirement: RestRecoverHealthWhenWoundClosed

#### Scenario: HealthRecoversWhenWoundsAreClosed
- **GIVEN** 0 bleed stacks and a health pool with Current 30 of Max 100

- **THEN** The health pool MUST recover to 40

#### Scenario: HealthRecoveryPreventedByOpenBleedWounds
- **GIVEN** 2 bleed stacks and a health pool with Current 30 of Max 100
- **WHEN** restRecoverHealthWhenWoundClosed is called with amount 10
- **THEN** The health pool Current value MUST remain untouched at 30

### Requirement: CreateAmbush

The system SHALL the system shall generate a monster pack ambush encounter scaled to the floor theme and player progression level.

#### Scenario: PrologueFloorAmbushGeneration
- **GIVEN** A floor theme with monsters and floorNum <= 1
- **WHEN** createAmbush is invoked
- **THEN** An AmbushData record is returned with a novice leader and novice minions configured to avoid early
player one-shots

#### Scenario: MidTierFloorAmbushGeneration
- **GIVEN** A floor theme with monsters and floorNum between 2 and 3
- **WHEN** createAmbush is invoked
- **THEN** An AmbushData record is returned featuring a veteran skirmisher leader accompanied by novice minions

#### Scenario: HighTierVanguardAmbushGeneration
- **GIVEN** A floor theme with monsters and floorNum >= 4
- **WHEN** createAmbush is invoked
- **THEN** An AmbushData record is returned featuring an elite master brute leading seasoned skirmishers
