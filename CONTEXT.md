# Fornach Domain Context

Fornach is a multi-plane tactical combat simulation and balance workbench modeling Physical and Mental conflict through uncapped stat progression, bounded ratio opposed dice pools, and dynamic entropy meters.

## Combat Planes & Vectors

### Physical Plane
The conflict domain governing somatic trauma, kinetic force, and bodily posture.

**Force**:
Offensive Physical Power attribute representing raw kinetic output and shield-breaking impact.
_Avoid_: Might, Strength

**Fortitude**:
Defensive Physical Power attribute representing structural mass and blunt impact absorption.
_Avoid_: Vigor, Constitution, Armor

**Finesse**:
Offensive Physical Agility attribute governing precision, strike velocity, and tempo exploitation.
_Avoid_: Dexterity, Speed

**Reflex**:
Defensive Physical Agility attribute governing active deflection windows and spatial evasion.
_Avoid_: Dodge, Evasion

**Prowess**:
Offensive Martial Discipline attribute governing weaponcraft, stance dissection, and tactical observation.
_Avoid_: Technique, Skill

**Poise**:
Defensive Martial Discipline attribute governing physical balance, equilibrium, and adrenaline regulation.
_Avoid_: Balance, Stability

### Mental Plane
The conflict domain governing cognitive fatigue, psychological authority, and social deconstruction.

**Intellect**:
Offensive Mental Power attribute representing authoritative command presence and psychic projection.
_Avoid_: Presence, Command, Charisma

**Resolve**:
Defensive Mental Power attribute representing internal conviction, psychological resilience, and moral defiance.
_Avoid_: Will, Defiance, Nerve

**Acuity**:
Offensive Mental Agility attribute representing speed of thought, rhetorical flair, and social subterfuge.
_Avoid_: Guile, Charm, Cunning

**Intuition**:
Defensive Mental Agility attribute representing instinctive situational awareness and deceptive tell detection.
_Avoid_: Insight, Scrutiny, Empathy

**Acumen**:
Offensive Mental Discipline attribute representing procedural mastery, legal technicalities, and social leverage.
_Avoid_: Leverage, Wit, Tactics

**Composure**:
Defensive Mental Discipline attribute representing emotional equilibrium, poker face stoicism, and taunt resistance.
_Avoid_: Stoicism, Calm, Restraint

## Tactical Timing & Tempo

**Vor**:
The tactical state of holding the initiative and driving the offensive exchange.
_Avoid_: Initiative, Proactive phase

**Nach**:
The tactical state of reacting, parrying, or absorbing an opponent's committed action.
_Avoid_: Reactive phase, After

**Indes**:
The simultaneous midpoint interception where a defender in the Nach exploits an attacker's commitment to seize the Vor.
_Avoid_: Counter-attack, Interrupt

---

## Status Meters & Thresholds

**Recklessness**:
A dynamic entropy meter (0–100) representing exposure, overextension, and loss of defensive discipline.
_Avoid_: Overextension, Heat, Strain

**Exhaustion**:
A physical depletion meter (0–100) inflicted by heavy Power strikes against Fortitude.
_Avoid_: Fatigue, Stamina burn

**Overwhelm**:
A kinetic trauma meter (0–100) inflicted by rapid Agility cadence against Reflex.
_Avoid_: Bleed, Hemorrhage

**Frustration**:
A tactical erosion meter (0–100) inflicted by Discipline pressure and disarms against Poise.
_Avoid_: Broken guard, Stance break

**Cognitive Fatigue**:
A mental depletion meter (0–100) inflicted by Intellect projection against Resolve.
_Avoid_: Mental burn, Morale break

**Confusion**:
A cognitive disorientation meter (0–100) inflicted by rapid Acuity strikes against Intuition.
_Avoid_: Disorientation, Static

**Provoke**:
A targeted agitation meter (0–100) inflicted by Acumen mockery against Composure.
_Avoid_: Loss of face, Enrage

**Collapse**:
A critical threshold reached at 100% on any meter, causing a 75% defense reduction and exposing the combatant to an immediate Execute Strike.
_Avoid_: Stagger, Break, Stun

**Execute Strike**:
A definitive lethal finisher targeted at a Collapsed combatant.
_Avoid_: Finisher, Coup de grâce

## Combat Dynamics & Swarm Mechanics

**Encirclement**:
A compounding defensive penalty suffered when defending against multiple sequential attacks in a single round.
_Avoid_: Flanking penalty, Gang-up bonus

**Cleave**:
Physical area-of-effect damage splashing to adjacent secondary combatants during heavy strikes.
_Avoid_: Sweep, Whirlwind

**Psychic Shockwave**:
Mental area-of-effect morale resonance splashing secondary targets during high-disparity mental strikes.
_Avoid_: Psychic splash, Mind blast

**Combat Tier**:
The five-rank mastery classification (Novice, Adept, Veteran, Master, GrandMaster) governing stat baselines, floor hits, and swarm resilience.
_Avoid_: Level, Rank

**Tactical Preparation**:
A consumable or tactical deployable modifying turn dynamics and countering swarm or tempo advantages.
_Avoid_: Item, Buff, Gadget

---

## Spatial Domain & Vision

**Vision Cone**:
A parametric visual field defined by origin, facing direction, viewing arc (30° to 360°), and distance cutoff.
_Avoid_: Sight line, Vision wedge

**Tunnel Vision**:
The constriction of the vision cone into a narrow forward arc caused by sprinting or elevated combat entropy.
_Avoid_: Adrenaline eye, Visual funnel

**Dilation**:
The gradual widening of a stationary combatant's vision cone back to 360° awareness, accelerated by Poise and Composure.
_Avoid_: Focus recovery, Field expansion

**Dijkstra Map**:
A multi-source scalar distance field radiating from designated goals for concurrent navigation and pursuit.
_Avoid_: Distance grid, Heatmap
